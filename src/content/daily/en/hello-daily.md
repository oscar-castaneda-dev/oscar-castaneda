This is the first note in **daily** — a micro blog for the small things that
never grow into a full post: a command worth remembering, a paragraph from
something I read, a rough idea I want out of my head.

Notes are short by design. No hero image, no reading time, no table of
contents. If a note keeps growing, it probably wants to be a
[blog post](/en/blog/) instead.
